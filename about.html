MEET THE TEAM

BRYSTAL MCKEE

Brystal founded McKee Media after a decade in corporate digital media and content strategy, leading digital content strategy for a global technology company before deciding to build something of her own. She started as a freelance commercial photographer in 2022, and quickly realized that long-term success meant building a full brand, strategy, and team around that work — which became McKee Media. Today she leads strategy for every McKee Media client and serves on the board of Women in Communications Austin (WCA).

CHRISTOPHER MCKEE

Chris leads content creation and video production for McKee Media's clients — and manages the network of videographers, photographers, and graphic designers who bring that creative work to life. He's a licensed commercial (FAA Part 107 certified) drone pilot serving Austin, Leander, Cedar Park, and Georgetown — from real estate listings and roof inspections to event coverage and promotional video, shot in 4K and HD.

TRISH BOYD

Trish is the operational backbone of McKee Media, building and managing the internal systems, workflows, and scalable processes that keep every client engagement running on time and on track. From onboarding to reporting and billing, she makes sure the business behind the business never slows down the strategy and creative work clients actually see — so every account gets a smooth, professional experience from day one.

MAYA WALKER-REICH

Maya is the day-to-day engine behind every McKee Media account, managing content calendars, coordinating social media scheduling, and keeping projects moving from strategy through to publish. She leads community management across client platforms — responding to comments, messages, and reviews — turning followers into engaged audiences and making sure no client conversation ever goes unanswered.

ARALYN VASQUEZ

Aralyn manages paid media campaigns and performance reporting for McKee Media's clients, working across Google Ads, Meta Ads, and GA4. She translates campaign data into clear, actionable insight — making sure every client's marketing budget is working as hard as it can.
